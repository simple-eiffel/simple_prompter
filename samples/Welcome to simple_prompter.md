# Welcome to simple_prompter

[CUE: press Control Alt P to start reading]

This is a practice script. Read it out loud at an easy pace, and the text scrolls for you. For now it moves at a steady speed. Following your voice comes in the next version.

To pause, press Control Alt Space. The text stops, and the start of the sentence you were on lights up. Press it again, and after a short count-in you pick up from that sentence.

[CUE: try pausing here, then go again]

If you stumble, press Control Alt Backspace. That is the Again key. It takes you back to the start of the sentence and counts you in.

While the text is paused, you can click any word to start from there. The mouse wheel steps back and forward a sentence at a time. A right click starts reading again.

If the text runs too fast or too slow, press Control Alt Up or Control Alt Down. Your speed is remembered.

## Writing your own scripts

A script is a plain text file or a Markdown file. Blank lines separate paragraphs, and every sentence is a place you can restart from.

A line that starts with a hash mark is a heading. You may read it aloud, but nothing requires you to.

A note in square brackets that starts with the word cue is a reminder to yourself. It shows dimmed, and you never say it.

Formatting is fine too. This sentence has **bold words** and *italic words* in it, and only the words show.

## Opening a script

Use the Open script button on the control window, press Control Alt O from anywhere, or drop a file on the control window. The next time you start, your last script opens again.
