## Performance findings (2026-10-05)
- Aligner: 0.11 ms/update lean, 2.5 ms/update with contracts (580 updates). Not a bottleneck.
- The suite's slowness (168-224 s DBC, 36 s lean) was ONE torn-JSONL-line replay: ISE's
  JSON_PARSER.next_json_string never checks end of input, so {"t":"hol looped to INTEGER overflow
  (157,779 ms with contracts, ~34 s lean, 4.28 GB working set observed). Real product risk: reopening
  a session after a crash would freeze. Fixed upstream in simple_json (unterminated_string_at guard
  in parse/is_valid_json; its disabled test_unclosed_string re-enabled; simple_json 273/273).
  After the fix: torn line 0.025 ms; suite 13.8 s DBC / 1.4 s lean.
- GC-off experiment: 30x slower (paging). Reverted.
- Tooling trap: client incremental builds do not pick up library edits; needed -clean.
