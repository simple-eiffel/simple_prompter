## Defects found by verification, and fixed (all behavior; no frozen contract changed)
Found by replaying the real recording larry_read_01 through the attempt aligner and the whole
analyzer. Synthetic Phase 4 tests had passed over every one of them.

1. Misreads were never flagged (T18 criterion unmet). PT_ATTEMPT_ALIGNER now records one-for-one
   substitutions (PT_MISREAD, last_misreads); PT_FLAGGER.flag_misreads flags those inside the
   final video; PT_SESSION_ANALYZER calls it. On larry_read_01: exactly one misread,
   "you said '55070' (script: '5070')"; homophones and "26" for "twenty twenty-six" are not flagged.
2. "55070" matched "5070": the phonetic key collapses repeated characters. Sound-alike keys now
   apply only to words without digits.
3. A long [CUE] line stopped the attempt aligner for the rest of the recording (cue words counted
   toward the 8-word lookahead): 102 of 252 heard words aligned. Cue words are now skipped and not
   counted; 214 of 252 align (the rest: a 37-word ad-lib, an ASR quirk, recovery after the skip).
4. A stop word in an ad-lib ("So") jumped the script four words ahead. Only a content word (or a
   multi-word token) may skip ahead now.
5. Multi-word equivalences were never matched by the attempt aligner ("for example" for "e.g.",
   "post condition" for "postcondition", "cpp" for "c p p"). Added (pair_match, match_width).
6. Lost recovery: after 2 unmatched words the search widens (content words, confirmed by the next
   heard word, so an ad-lib word cannot pull the pointer), and the unmatched words just before a
   recovered match are aligned backward (retro_indices).
7. One garbled word discarded a whole passage: the solver required every word heard. Now every
   word must exist in the revision read (no stale takes) and all but one in five must be heard.
   The numbers passage (and its misread) came back; missing words on larry_read_01 fell from 50
   to exactly the 30 words of the paragraph the cue said to skip.
8. emit_cut stretched a cut to the start of the whole attempt when a passage's first word was
   unheard. Spans now come from the first and last words actually heard.
9. A cue line between passages split one continuous read into two cuts. Passages with nothing to
   read no longer break a run.
10. Neighbouring cuts of one take overlapped by ~1 s (audio played twice). The snapper now makes
    them meet between the last word of one and the first word of the next (in silence when there
    is one; tight otherwise).
11. The snapper probed only 20 ms past a word edge; VAD speech runs past whisper word ends, so
    good cuts were marked tight and padded instead of placed in silence. It now searches for the
    nearest silence within Max_lead / Max_tail (silence_before, silence_after).
12. A 12-second ad-lib inside one take was kept in the final video. Runs now split where more than
    Max_join_gap (4 s) passes between the heard words of consecutive passages.

Whole analyzer on larry_read_01 (one take, 0-144 s), before -> after:
    cuts 6 (2 overlapping pairs) -> 3, none overlapping
    output 113.2 s (whole passages wrongly dropped) -> 125.0 s (all read passages kept, ad-lib removed)
    missing 50 words -> 30 (exactly the skipped paragraph)
    Misread flags 0 -> 1 (55070); Long_pause flags include the instructed 3 s pause
    tight cuts: 3 remain, all genuine (title read straight into the first sentence; "blah blah"
